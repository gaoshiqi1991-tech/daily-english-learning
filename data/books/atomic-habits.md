---
title: Atomic Habits - 1% Better Every Day
book: Atomic Habits (James Clear)
tags: [habit, self-improvement]
---
If you get just 1% better each day for one year, you will end up thirty-seven
times better by the end (1.01^365 ≈ 37.78). Conversely, getting 1% worse each
day brings you nearly to zero (0.99^365 ≈ 0.03). Habits are the compound
interest of self-improvement.

Clear's core advice: do not focus on goals, focus on systems. "You do not rise
to the level of your goals; you fall to the level of your systems."

Useful expression: "compound effect" — small changes, accumulated over time,
produce remarkable results.
