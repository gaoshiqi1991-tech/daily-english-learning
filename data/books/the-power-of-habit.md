---
title: The Power of Habit - Cue, Routine, Reward
book: The Power of Habit (Charles Duhigg)
tags: [habit, psychology]
---
Every habit follows a three-step loop: a cue (a trigger that tells the brain to
go into automatic mode), a routine (the behavior itself), and a reward (which
helps the brain decide whether the loop is worth remembering).

To change a habit, keep the same cue and reward, but replace the routine.
This is the "golden rule of habit change": you cannot extinguish a bad habit,
you can only change it.

Useful expression: "old habits die hard" — it is difficult to stop doing
something you have done for a long time.
