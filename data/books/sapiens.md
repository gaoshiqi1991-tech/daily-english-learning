---
title: Sapiens - The Cognitive Revolution
book: Sapiens (Yuval Noah Harari)
tags: [history, anthropology]
---
About 70,000 years ago, Homo sapiens underwent a Cognitive Revolution. What set
us apart was not individual intelligence but our unique ability to believe in
shared myths — fictions like nations, money, and human rights — which allow
millions of strangers to cooperate flexibly on a large scale.

Harari argues that "fiction" is therefore not a weakness of human society but
its very foundation: any large-scale human cooperation system is rooted in
common myths that exist only in our collective imagination.

Useful expression: "a double-edged sword" — something that has both favorable
and unfavorable consequences.
